# Use of AI

Team Earshot used Claude (Anthropic), an AI assistant, for brainstorming, research,
writing, and most of the code, including the data-preparation script and the data
files it generated from NASA and CSA sources. The team directed, reviewed and
tested all of it. The project's focus on astronaut hearing came from the team.

AI-written files are marked with a comment at the top; generated data files carry
an `aiProvenance` field. Earshot contains no AI-generated images, video or audio,
and no NASA branding.
